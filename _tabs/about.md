# About Me

I'm Juanes, a Systems and Computing Engineering student building a public learning log in cybersecurity.

This site documents my progress through CTFs, vulnerable labs, Linux, web pentesting, networking, and technical notes. The goal is not to publish inflated theory, but to keep a reproducible record of what I learn, what I break, what I fix, and what I understand along the way.

## What You'll Find Here

* CTF and vulnerable machine writeups.
* Notes on enumeration, exploitation, and privilege escalation.
* Useful Linux commands and workflows.
* Web pentesting notes.
* Personal cheatsheets.
* Security-related projects and experiments.

## Approach

This blog is a public technical notebook. It prioritizes clarity, reproducibility, and real learning.
