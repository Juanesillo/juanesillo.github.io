This blog is my personal cybersecurity learning log.

Here I will document CTFs, labs, mistakes, commands, methodology, and real technical learning. The goal is not to publish inflated theory, but to build reproducible evidence of what I am learning and how I am improving.

## What I Will Publish

- CTF and lab writeups.
- Linux notes.
- Web pentesting notes.
- Cheatsheets.
- Real mistakes and fixes.
- Personal security projects.

## Goal

To build a public technical knowledge base and show real progress in cybersecurity.
